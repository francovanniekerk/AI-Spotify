AI integration breakdown

How Spotify uses Intelligent systems to personalise listening

Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning Generative AI

Selected platform: Spotify

Spotify is a music and podcast streaming platform. Intelligent features help listeners discover content and enjoy a more personal listening experience.

How the concepts connect: AI is the broad field. Machine Learning is a part of AI that learns from data. Generative AI uses learned patterns to create content. These categories overlap, so a single feature can use all three.

01 / AI

Artificial Intelligence

AI DJ featurse

Spotify's DJ selects a stream of music for the listener and can change the selection when they request a different atmosphere.

Why it fits

Choosing suitable music is a task that normally involves human judgement. The DJ automates this task, making it an example of AI. It also uses ML and Generative AI.

Example: Tap the DJ button to switch to a different selection of music.

02 / ML

Machine Learning

Feature: Personalised recommendations

Spotify suggests music and podcasts based on information such as listening history, playlists and interactions with the platform.

Why it fits

ML models learn patterns from data to predict what a listener might enjoy. The suggestions are personalised rather than being the same fixed list for everyone.

Example: A listener who enjoys acoustic music might receive suggestions for similar artists.

03 / GEN AI

Generative AI

Feature: DJ commentary and voice

Spotify uses generative AI with its music editors to help produce DJ commentary. An AI voice system turns text into realistic speech.

Why it fits

This creates language and spoken audio, rather than only predicting which existing song to play. The generated content here is the commentary, not the music.

Example: The DJ introduces a selection with spoken information about the artists.
